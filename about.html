Our Story

Physiotherapy that's always within reach

PhysioSync started from a simple frustration: good physiotherapy is expensive, hard to schedule, and often ends the moment you leave the clinic. We built a way to bring real, camera-verified guidance home — private, accurate, and free to use.

Why We Built This

Recovery shouldn't stop at the clinic door

Most rehabilitation happens between appointments — at home, alone, guessing whether you're doing an exercise correctly. PhysioSync closes that gap with real pose-tracking, not a video you have to interpret yourself.

Real-time, not guesswork

Every rep is measured with actual joint-angle geometry from your camera feed — the same landmarks a physiotherapist would watch for — so form feedback reflects what your body is actually doing, not a generic animation.

Private by design

Your camera feed is processed entirely on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, recorded, or sent to a server — the same privacy standard we'd want for our own physiotherapy sessions.

What Guides Us

Built on clinical principles, not shortcuts

This is a medical-adjacent product, and we treat it that way — every exercise, score, and piece of feedback is designed to be something a physiotherapist would actually stand behind.

Accuracy first

No fake scores. If tracking is uncertain, we say so instead of showing a number we can't stand behind.

Beginner-safe

Every exercise in the library is intentionally gentle and low-risk — appropriate for home use without in-person supervision.

Free to use

No account paywalls, no subscription to access exercises. Recovery guidance shouldn't be gated behind a price tag.

Evidence-based

Our research library cites WHO data directly — every claim is traceable back to its source, not just asserted.
